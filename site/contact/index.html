What to Have Ready

If you're calling or emailing about a new property, having your building's approximate square footage, tenant count, and current cleaning arrangement (if any) ready speeds up the first conversation. If you're an existing client with a service issue, having the date and specific location of the issue helps us route it to the right crew immediately rather than spending the first exchange gathering details.

Existing Clients

If you're already working with us and have a same-day issue, such as a restroom supply shortage or a spill in a common area, call (866) 958-8773 directly rather than using the form below, since the form is routed for new quote requests and general inquiries.

Coordinating Across Multiple Properties

If you manage more than one building in the Peoria area and want a single point of contact across your portfolio, mention that when you reach out. We can coordinate scheduling, reporting, and billing across multiple properties under one relationship rather than treating each building as a separate account with its own onboarding process and its own vendor contact.
