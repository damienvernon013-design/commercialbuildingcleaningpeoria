Combining Services

Most buildings don't need just one of these services in isolation. A common structure pairs common-area cleaning with a restroom program under one scope of work, and larger or higher-traffic buildings add day porter coverage to close the gap between scheduled visits. We build one written scope covering whatever combination fits your property rather than requiring separate contracts per service.

How We Scope Service to Your Building

Every engagement starts with a walkthrough, not a phone-quoted estimate. We look at your building's layout, tenant mix, and traffic pattern, then put together a written scope specific to what you actually need. A single-story medical suite in Sun City gets scoped differently than a multi-story office building near Loop 101, because the traffic pattern, restroom core count, and seasonal exposure are genuinely different.
