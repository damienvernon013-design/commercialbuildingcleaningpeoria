Multi-Tenant Restroom Programs in Peoria, AZ

Shared restroom cores in multi-tenant buildings take more traffic than a single-office restroom, and running out of supplies or falling behind on disinfection shows up fast in tenant complaints. We coordinate scheduled restroom programs sized to your building's tenant count and traffic pattern.

What's Included

  • Fixture cleaning and disinfection, with touchpoint focus on door handles, faucet handles, and stall latches
  • Supply restocking: paper products, soap, and sanitizer, checked and logged on every visit
  • Floor cleaning and disinfection
  • Trash removal
  • ADA fixture checks where applicable, including grab-bar security and accessible clearance

Frequency Options

Standard multi-tenant buildings typically run daily or every-other-day restroom service. Buildings with medical or healthcare tenants usually need a tighter rotation with documented touchpoint disinfection; we scope that specifically rather than applying a generic office-building schedule to a clinical tenant mix.

Who This Suits

Multi-tenant office buildings, medical office buildings, and mixed-use properties with shared restroom facilities. This runs as a standalone service or combined with common-area cleaning and day porter coverage.

Why This Runs on a Different Standard Than a Single-Office Restroom

A restroom shared across several tenants sees traffic patterns a single office never does: back-to-back use during business hours, higher supply consumption, and a wider range of expectations from tenants who each notice different things. What one tenant considers an acceptable supply level, another considers a running-out problem, and in a shared facility, that judgment gets made by every tenant using it, not just one. That's why the program runs on a documented schedule with logged supply checks rather than a "restock when someone notices" approach.

How We Scope This for Your Building

We assess your restroom core count, tenant mix, and traffic pattern during the initial walkthrough, then write a scope specifying check frequency, touchpoint disinfection detail, and supply restocking standards by name rather than a general description. Buildings with medical or healthcare tenants get a tighter rotation with documented disinfection protocol built in from the start, not added after a complaint.

Who This Suits

Multi-tenant office buildings, medical office buildings, and mixed-use properties with shared restroom facilities. This runs as a standalone service or combined with common-area cleaning and day porter coverage.

Documentation for Healthcare and Compliance-Sensitive Tenants

Buildings with medical or healthcare tenants often need documentation of restroom disinfection for their own compliance records, not just a clean appearance. We log supply checks and disinfection passes on every visit for buildings that need that documentation, so a property manager or tenant can point to a written record rather than relying on visual inspection alone, which matters if a compliance question ever comes up.

ADA and Accessibility Considerations

In buildings with an older-adult or mobility-aid-dependent tenant base, particularly common in the Sun City and Sun City West portions of our service area, restroom service includes checking that grab bars remain secure, accessible stall clearance isn't blocked by supply storage, and fixture heights meet the standard the building was built to. These checks are built into the standard visit rather than treated as a separate compliance inspection, and any issue found gets flagged to the property manager the same day rather than waiting for the next scheduled report.

Available Across the Service Area

Restroom programs run across Peoria and our service area, including the medical office clusters in Sun City and Sun City West.
