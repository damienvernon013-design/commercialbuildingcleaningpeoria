About Peoria Building Cleaning Co

Peoria Building Cleaning Co has spent 22 years coordinating commercial cleaning coverage for office buildings, medical buildings, and mixed-use properties across Peoria, Arizona and the surrounding West Valley. We work with property managers, building engineers, and REIT asset managers who need a single point of contact for common-area cleaning, multi-tenant restroom programs, and day porter coverage, without managing multiple vendor relationships across a portfolio.

We route service requests to insured, bonded local crews sized to the building, whether that is a single-story medical suite on Grand Avenue or a multi-story office building near Loop 101. Every crew works from a written scope of work agreed before the first clean, so tenants and ownership know exactly what is covered.

What We Focus On

Commercial buildings in the Peoria area deal with conditions a generic cleaning contract does not always account for: monsoon-season dust on entry glass, extreme summer heat that stresses HVAC intake grilles and accelerates exterior soiling, and a tenant mix that in places like Sun City and Sun City West skews toward medical offices serving an older-adult population. We scope service around the building, not a one-size template.

How We Work

Every engagement starts with a walkthrough of your property, not a phone-quoted number based on square footage alone. From there we put together a written scope of work covering exactly what's included, at what frequency, and what our response-time commitment is. That document becomes the reference point for both sides for the life of the engagement, which matters when a tenant complaint or a CAM reconciliation question comes up months later.

Who We Work With

Our clients are property managers overseeing single buildings or small portfolios, building engineers responsible for day-to-day facility condition, and REIT asset managers who need consistent, documented service across properties in this market without managing each vendor relationship individually. If that describes your role, our process is built around the accountability and documentation your position requires.
