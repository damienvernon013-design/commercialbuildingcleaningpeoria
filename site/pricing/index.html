Commercial Building Cleaning Pricing in Peoria, AZ

Commercial cleaning pricing depends on square footage, the number of tenants sharing common areas, service frequency, and the specific scope: common-area cleaning, restroom programs, day porter coverage, or a combination. Because building stock in the Peoria area ranges from single-story medical suites on Grand Avenue to multi-story office buildings near Loop 101, a flat per-square-foot rate rarely reflects what a specific property actually needs.

What Drives Your Price

  • Square footage and layout. A single-tenant standalone building scopes differently than a multi-tenant complex with shared corridors.
  • Tenant mix. Medical and healthcare tenants typically require more frequent restroom service and touchpoint disinfection than a standard professional office.
  • Frequency. Nightly, every-other-day, and weekly schedules price differently, and some buildings mix frequencies across services.
  • Day porter coverage. On-site daytime staffing is quoted separately from after-hours cleaning.

Getting an Accurate Number

Pricing varies by square footage, space type, and frequency. The most accurate way to get a number for your property is a written quote based on your building's actual scope. Request a written quote and we'll follow up with pricing matched to your property, not a generic range that doesn't reflect what you actually need.

Why We Don't Publish a Flat Rate

A single-tenant office building with one shared lobby costs less to service than a multi-tenant building at the same square footage with several shared restroom cores and higher common-area traffic. A per-square-foot number that ignores that difference either overcharges the simpler building or undercharges the more complex one, and neither outcome serves you. Instead, every quote is built from your building's actual layout and tenant mix.

What to Have Ready When You Ask for a Quote

To get an accurate number quickly, have your building's approximate square footage, tenant count, current restroom core count, and any known scheduling constraints ready. If you have an existing cleaning contract, having that scope on hand helps us identify gaps or overlaps rather than starting from zero.
