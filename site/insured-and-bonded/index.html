Insured, Bonded and 22 years Serving Peoria

Commercial buildings carry liability exposure that a homeowner's cleaning arrangement doesn't, which is why every crew we coordinate work through carries general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote, so your property management company or ownership group has documentation on file before service starts.

Bonding

Crews working in your building's common areas and shared spaces are bonded, providing an added layer of protection appropriate for multi-tenant commercial properties.

That track record means we've built relationships with crews who understand what a multi-tenant office building, a medical suite, or a light-industrial property actually needs, rather than applying a residential-cleaning mindset to a commercial contract.

Documentation for Your Property File

If your property management company requires a certificate of insurance on file before granting building access, request one along with your written quote and we'll include it as part of onboarding.

Why This Matters More in a Multi-Tenant Building

A cleaning crew working in a multi-tenant building's shared spaces carries different liability exposure than one working in a single-tenant suite. Shared lobbies, elevator interiors, and restroom cores see traffic from every tenant in the building, not just one, which means insurance coverage appropriate for that exposure matters more, not less, than it would for a standalone office. We treat that distinction as part of how we scope every multi-tenant engagement.

If your property file requires those specifics, they're provided directly with your certificate of insurance rather than posted publicly where they serve no purpose beyond appearing complete.
