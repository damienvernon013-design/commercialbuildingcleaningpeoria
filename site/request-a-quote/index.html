Request a Free Cleaning Quote in Peoria

Tell us about your building and what needs covering, and we will follow up with a written quote. We reply next business day, every business day.

What We Need From You

  • Approximate square footage and number of tenants
  • Which services you're evaluating: common area, restrooms, day porter, or a combination
  • Current cleaning frequency, if any, and where the gaps are

What Happens Next

  1. We review your request and, if useful, schedule a walkthrough of the property.
  2. We put together a written scope of work matched to your building.
  3. You get a quote with clear pricing, not a vague estimate.

Our Response-Time Commitment

Our office hours are Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm. Requests submitted during or outside those hours get a reply the next business day, in writing. We don't leave quote requests unanswered for a week while you wait to hear whether your building is even in our service area; if there's a coverage question, we'll tell you directly rather than letting the request go quiet.

Quoting a Portfolio of Buildings

If you're requesting a quote for more than one property, note that in your submission. We scope each building individually since square footage, tenant mix, and traffic pattern vary by property, but we coordinate the quoting and onboarding process across your whole portfolio under one point of contact rather than treating each building as a separate transaction with its own back-and-forth and its own paperwork.
